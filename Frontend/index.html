Our Story

Rooted in Multan, Inspired by Pakistan's Textile Heritage

Faisalabad Textile Depot is a Multan-based clothing store dedicated to bringing customers high-quality unstitched fabrics for shalwar kameez, formal wear, and everyday essentials. We carefully source our collections from trusted textile manufacturers across Pakistan.

Our focus has always been simple: provide quality fabrics, honest pricing, and a wide selection of stylish designs.
